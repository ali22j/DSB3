# Solar Energy Assessment in Bahrain

## Project Overview
This project aims to support solar energy development in Bahrain using satellite imagery and artificial intelligence.

The project consists of two main components:

1. **Solar Site Suitability:** Analyzes satellite imagery to identify potential areas in Bahrain that may be suitable for future solar energy projects.
2. **Solar Panel Detection:** Uses a deep learning model (YOLO26m-seg) to detect and segment existing solar panels in images.

## Project Objective
The main objective is to provide an initial assessment of potential solar energy locations and identify existing solar installations, helping support better planning and monitoring of solar energy development in Bahrain.

## Technologies Used
- Python
- Google Earth Engine
- Sentinel-2 Satellite Imagery
- YOLO26m-seg
- Streamlit

## Expected Outcome
A combined approach that provides a solar site suitability map and an AI-based solar panel detection model.

## Limitations
The suitability results are preliminary and require further validation using additional factors such as land availability, accessibility, and grid connectivity.
